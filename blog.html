Understanding Your Skin Type

Knowing your skin type is the first step to effective skincare. Here’s a quick guide to help you identify yours:

Oily Skin

Characterized by a persistent shine, enlarged pores, and a tendency for blackheads and breakouts. Oily skin produces excess sebum. Look for lightweight, non-comedogenic products like our Refreshing Gel Cleanser and oil-free moisturizers.

Dry Skin

Feels tight, rough, and may look flaky or dull. Dry skin lacks natural oils (lipids). It needs rich, nourishing products to replenish moisture. Our Nourishing Cleansing Balm and Overnight Repair Night Cream are excellent choices.

Combination Skin

A mix of both oily and dry, typically with an oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) and drier cheeks. The key is balance. Use gentle cleansers and target different areas with different products, such as a hydrating serum on cheeks and a lighter moisturizer on the T-zone.

Sensitive Skin

Easily irritated, prone to redness, itching, or stinging. Sensitive skin requires gentle, fragrance-free products. Always patch-test new products. Our Gentle Foaming Cleanser and Hydrating Rose Toner are formulated to be kind to sensitive skin.

How to Use Our Products: A Simple Skincare Routine

Building a routine doesn't have to be complicated. Here is a foundational 4-step routine for morning and night:

Start with a clean slate. Use a cleanser appropriate for your skin type to remove dirt, oil, and makeup. For a deep clean, try double cleansing in the evening, starting with an oil or balm cleanser.

After cleansing, apply a toner to rebalance your skin's pH and prepare it for the next steps. Our Hydrating Rose Toner is a great universal option.

This is where you target specific concerns. Apply a serum like our Radiance Vitamin C Serum (for brightening) or Hyaluronic Acid Serum (for hydration).

Lock in all the goodness with a moisturizer. This step is crucial for all skin types—yes, even oily skin! It hydrates and protects your skin barrier.
